# 360p pixel mode — v260

Enable **Settings > Graphics > 360P PIXEL MODE**, then **Apply & Save**.
The mode is off by default. Its preference is stored separately from the normal
render-scale, AA and blur settings, so those choices survive switching back.

## Rendering

The existing runtime URP asset renders game cameras at 360 internal lines,
preserving aspect ratio, then uses URP's explicit **Point** upscaler. The window
resolution and camera projection remain intact. Screen-space menus, subtitles
and HUDs are drawn at display resolution.

MSAA, camera AA and final dithering are suppressed during the mode, along with
motion blur, depth of field and film grain. Other lighting, colour and gameplay
settings are retained. Camera and volume state is restored when disabled or when
the owning settings menu unloads. New game cameras and loaded-scene volumes are
handled without per-frame scene searches. Only runtime copies of pipeline and
volume assets are used.

The two existing world-space UI captions (Nailong's health/name label and the
placed rifle caption) mirror their text into a native-resolution overlay in this
mode. They retain projected size/position, colour, parent opacity and a throttled
physics occlusion check; the original text is restored on exit. The 3D world and
the world-space health-bar geometry remain pixel rendered.

The render-scale, AA and motion-blur controls indicate the override and are
disabled while the draft pixel mode is selected. The new row is inserted into
the existing scrollable menu at runtime, covering both main and pause menus
without reserializing scenes or prefabs.
