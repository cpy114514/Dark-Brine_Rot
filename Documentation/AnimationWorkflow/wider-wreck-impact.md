# Wider tail-strike wreck scatter — October 13, 2026

The user found the opening fight cluttered because ship fragments remain too close together. The tail strike now gives the original ship cuts a stronger, divergent initial launch. No boards are placed, resized, moved apart after landing, or added to make a shot work.

The force changes are in `Assets/Scripts/Story/Chapter1/Story1WreckBattle.cs`:

- `shipBreakPower` defaults to 1.35 (runtime clamped to 1–3).
- The previous directional horizontal launch is multiplied by that power.
- Each source gets an additional outward component from the actual original deck-centre bounds, with random speed of 12–22 m/s multiplied by `(power - 1)`. At the default this adds 4.2–7.7 m/s to spread the hull rather than translate an intact cluster.
- Upward launch increases by 8.75% at the default, so the stronger horizontal movement has time to separate before water drag slows it.
- Initial shards scatter away from their own original parent-cut centre, with separation multiplied by `1 + 2 * (power - 1)` (1.7 at default). This prevents a parent cut's wooden strips from travelling as a tight bundle. Later shark attacks on individual boards retain their existing impulses and separation.
- Power 1 retains the previous launch formula and random-sample order for the comparison fixture.

Gravity, mass/inertia, collision meshes, continuous collision detection, water resistance, buoyancy/settling, random release, real tail contact cue, ship-fracture timing, player handback, existing animations and letterboxing are preserved. There is no controllable surfing, new VFX or audio.
