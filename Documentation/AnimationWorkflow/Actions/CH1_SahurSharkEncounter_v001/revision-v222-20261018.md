## Behavior

- Attack prompt approaches with the native Swim cycle instead of holding a raised Bite_Lunge pose. The snout sits higher with a slight upward pitch so the face reads above the waves. Tutorial counter contact fits the forward snout surface to the actual stick shaft rather than pulling an arbitrary cheek vertex around the player. Hit registration follows final body separation, so the measured contact describes the rendered pose.
- Ctrl uses the current stance, native cut deck and current surrounding compound timber to select a supported route. Candidates favor lateral distance from the committed bite. Twelve samples check deck coverage and the measured body envelope against forecast timber movement. It chooses the first completely validated candidate in preference order. This is a runtime selection, not a fixed left/right endpoint.
- The roll follows the moving board, stays at its destination, and transitions once to Guard. The shark attacks the pre-roll position and retreats before the Charge prompt. A native posed-shark triangle tree checks all thirteen measured hero body capsules, including head, limbs and feet, and moves the shark out of that envelope. A conservative plane separation handles failure to converge. The last Charge entry pose is checked again.
- Swimming requires at least six seconds of actual swimming input and eight meters of measured travel before cinematic pursuit can claim control. No suitable boarding target leaves normal player control active while searching. Pause/map input does not count toward this time. All prompts remain English.
