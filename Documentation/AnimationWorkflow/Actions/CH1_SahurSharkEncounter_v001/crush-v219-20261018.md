## Changes

- Replaced the abrupt rising/falling direction switch with a continuous pitch change. Removed the frozen apex position while retaining the approved short slow-motion interval.
- The underside follows a continuous arc. The shark starts farther outside the ship, gains height and crosses toward the deck. Its real posed underside still supplies hull contact; visible shark size is unchanged.
- Remapped the authored clip so its 2.52s body compression occurs at the hull strike. Previously contact sampled the clip's recovered end pose.
- Continued the actual impact nose/root trajectory into a downward follow-through and swimming recovery. The old one-frame root jump was 44.316m at 15 FPS sampling. The revised take19 contact transition was 5.513m between frames, consistent with neighboring fast descent samples (5.156m, 5.430m), followed by decelerating steps (4.223m, 3.044m).
- Added short entry/recovery bone blends and mixed in the existing Swim tail stroke and a small fin contribution. The original v214 action used a fixed Swim frame as its base. Its authored spine compression is preserved; tail effort is strongest at takeoff, reduced in air and restored on re-entry. No rest pose or source FBX was overwritten.
- Reframed descent to retain the full animal in view rather than cropping its upper silhouette at the cut.

## Performance work

Native geometry remains the contact basis. Changes do not reduce sweep sample coverage or replace visible meshes with larger invisible collision boxes.

- Spatial trees reject pairs of unrelated regions before precise triangle tests. Cached bounds avoid repeated triangle-bound construction.
- Sweep bounds interpolate endpoint minima/maxima conservatively while exact tests use the current intermediate vertices.
- An unreleased cut cell must be a candidate before querying its entire original rigging mesh. This avoids expensive whole-source queries for empty local regions.
- Static timber geometry reuses its transforms and bounds until they change. Rigging setup is split over frames ahead of the leap.
- Shark tree topology is prepared incrementally during the voyage, then refitted to the actual posed skin. The first breach frame no longer builds the entire tree synchronously.
- Expensive per-vertex cloth transfer auditing now runs only when explicit geometry diagnostics are enabled. Ordinary gameplay retains the same barycentric transfer without the review-only closest-surface search. Zero in that diagnostic field with diagnostics disabled means **not measured**, not proof of zero error.
