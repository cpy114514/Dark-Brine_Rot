# Yellow shoreline sand

Source asset: [Aerial Sand](https://polyhaven.com/a/aerial_sand), by Rob Tuytel / Poly Haven.

License: [CC0](https://polyhaven.com/license). Downloaded on 2026-09-27.

Included original 2K diffuse and OpenGL normal maps. The warm yellow colour is a
material tint; the original texture files are unchanged.

The shoreline shader blends dry sand into existing grass and rocks, and darkens
the waterline to represent wet sand. No terrain geometry or collision was changed.
