SahurFastFreestyle is adapted from CMU Graphics Lab motion capture trial 125_06 (Free Style).
Source: http://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/search.php?subjectnumber=125
Terms: http://mocap.cs.cmu.edu/
BVH conversion by Bruce Hahne (cgspeed MotionBuilder-friendly release, 2010); mirror: https://github.com/Shriinivas/cmubvh

CMU permits including the data in commercially sold products, but does not permit reselling the data directly, including converted data. The BVH conversion has no additional restrictions.

The data used in this project was obtained from mocap.cs.cmu.edu.
The database was created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217.

Adaptation: humanoid retargeting, loop extraction, removal of translation and heading drift, height alignment, faster cadence, and flutter kick replacing the studio support pose.
Source and reproducible import tool: Tools/AnimationSources/CMU and Tools/ImportSahurFreestyle.cs.
